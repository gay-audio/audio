# Ideology

In recent years, countries have been increasingly signing laws criminalising victimless actions, and these laws are then used as a pretext for more digital surveillance. 

Social media platforms have shown themselves willing to censor content related to: minority activism, sexuality, sexual health, mental health.

Make no mistake, these things are not coincidences. The centralised nature of these organisations creates the opportunity for people to abuse their power. A strong government will not prosecute rapists: more often than not, it will allow them into its highest ranks. A centralised social media website will not fight racism: more often than not, it will silence activists for "going too far".

[NAME OF PROJECT] exists to be the antithesis to this trend.

There is no centralised body that can delete your content. In fact, there is no centralised body at all. Content is hosted by content creators and distributed through content hubs that enforce their own community standards. 

This will be more difficult than traditional platforms. Self-hosting is complicated, as it requires technical knowledge you might not otherwise have. But when the state tightens its grip, every autonomy you have will be tested to the limit, and you will be grateful for every last bit.

## Creator

> Why did you decide to make this?

I am a Trans, Lesbian, Autistic Woman, dating a Trans, Lesbian, Disabled, Autistic, Irish/Cantonese Woman, and above all else, I am an Anarchist. 

I have seen legally acceptable violence performed towards those like me and those I love my entire life. 

- Alan Turing was legally driven to suicide by the British government.
- ...

I will always exist semi-legally. What laws I do follow, I do so because I believe them to be good; what laws I do not consider to be good, I ignore. I hold no stock in the rule of law, the states monopoly on violence, nor any other machine of subjugation.

Recent laws have seen governments around the world increasingly revoke the privacy and autonomy of people online. When these laws are used to do harm (and they **will**), I, and those I love, will be among the first hurt.
